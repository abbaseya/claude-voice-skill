```
A few thoughts on why building reliable AI systems matters more than ever.

In today's fast-paced digital landscape, organizations are increasingly relying
on AI to drive transformation. Here's what I've learned: the reality is that most
AI deployments fail not because of model quality, but because of organizational
alignment. Furthermore, without proper governance, even the best models will
underperform.

Let me know your thoughts in the comments!
```

Diagnosis:
- "A few thoughts on" and "Let me know your thoughts in the comments" announce the
  writing instead of being the writing. Both are openers and closers that belong to
  no author in particular.
- Buzzword stack: "fast-paced digital landscape", "drive transformation",
  "organizational alignment". None of them say anything that could be checked.
- "Here's what I've learned" / "the reality is" / "Furthermore" are formulaic
  connective tissue standing in for an actual transition.
- Nothing here is falsifiable. There is no number, no example, and no claim that
  could turn out to be wrong.
