# Your own typo corrections, for `harvest_sessions.py --typos <this file>`.
#
# One per line, `wrong=right`. Lines starting with # are ignored. Matching is
# whole-word and case-insensitive; the replacement keeps the case you typed.
#
# The harvester already carries a set of generic English keyboard slips — "wiht",
# "teh", "recieve" and friends. This file is for what it cannot know:
#
#   - product, tool and jargon names you mistype
#   - your own recurring transpositions
#   - anything specific to your work
#
# Two rules, both learned the hard way from a spellchecker's suggestions:
#
#   1. Never add a word that is also a real word. "wont", "lets", "ill", "id",
#      "its" and "apps" all look like typos in context and are not. Correcting
#      one rewrites a correct sentence into a broken one.
#   2. Never correct grammar or regional spelling here. "behaviour", "organised"
#      and "sceptic" are not errors. Neither is your idiom — dropped articles and
#      unusual agreement are a large part of what makes your prose yours rather
#      than the model's, and normalising them removes exactly the signal this
#      whole skill is trying to preserve.
#
